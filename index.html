Who is the
Imposter?

Pass the phone. Everyone gets the secret word, except one.

3 to 30 players · 1 imposter

How to play
  1. Each player holds the card to see their secret word, then passes the phone on.
  2. One random player is the imposter. They only get a vague hint.
  3. Take turns saying one word that relates to the secret word.
  4. Discuss and vote on who you think the imposter is.
  5. If the imposter survives the vote or guesses the word, they win.
